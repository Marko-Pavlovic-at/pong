# Phase 17 — Make it yours and wrap up

**Milestone E — Make it yours**

**Goal:** Finish with a distinct, documented game rather than a copy of the reference.

**Build:**

- One personal change that meaningfully affects the experience: a new visual theme, different court geometry, an original scoring twist, a unique bot personality, a different progression structure, or a custom sound palette.
- A `README.md` with how to run the game, the rules, the controls, and one paragraph about what you learned.
- A final full playthrough from level 1 to victory, plus a loss-and-retry test.
- A short reflection comparing your version with the reference and naming the decisions you made.

**Concepts:** product thinking, documentation, refactoring for clarity, scope control, publishing with a static host, explaining technical decisions in plain language, self-assessment.

**Decisions:** What makes your version recognizably yours? What will you deliberately leave out to finish well?

**Done when:**

- Someone else can run the game from your README.
- All core requirements still pass.
- Your version is visibly and behaviorally distinct from `pong-AI`.
- You can explain the major systems without opening the code.
- `progress.md` reflects every completed phase.

**Stretch:** Deploy it, add a local high-score table, or build a second bot personality.

**Watch out:** Adding new scope before the existing game is stable, and polishing visuals while leaving the core loop untested.
